# FlowRight Plumbing Website

A fast, mobile-friendly, single-page website for a plumbing business. Plain HTML/CSS/JS — no build step.

## View it
Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Make it yours
Search and replace these placeholders across `index.html`:

| Placeholder | Replace with |
|---|---|
| `FlowRight` / `FlowRight Plumbing` | Your business name |
| `(000) 000-0000` and `tel:+10000000000` | Your phone number |
| `hello@flowrightplumbing.com` | Your email |
| `Your City & surrounding areas` | Your service area |
| Reviews section | Real customer reviews |
| Stats (15+ years, 8,000+ jobs) | Your real numbers |

## Receive form submissions
The forms currently just show a thank-you message. To get submissions emailed to you, sign up at [Formspree](https://formspree.io), set each `<form>`'s `action` to your Formspree URL with `method="POST"`, and remove the form handler in `script.js`.
