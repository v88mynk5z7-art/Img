Visit the help centre
Do I need to speak to a doctor on video?

No. Most consultations are a short online questionnaire. If a clinician needs more information, they'll message you, and occasionally ask for a photo or a call.

What happens if I'm not suitable for a treatment?

You won't be charged. Your clinician will explain why and, where possible, suggest another option or recommend you see your GP.

Will my GP be told?

Only with your consent. For some medicines we strongly recommend it, so your records stay complete and safe.

Can I pause or cancel my plan?

Yes, at any time from your account. There are no minimum terms and no cancellation fees.
