The process

Clinical care, without the waiting room

From your first answer to your ongoing reviews, every step is designed by practising clinicians. Here is exactly what happens, and when.

  1. 1About 5 minutes

    Consultation

    Choose a treatment area and answer questions about your health, medicines and goals. You can save and return.

  2. 2Usually within 24 hours

    Clinical review

    A UK-registered prescriber reviews your answers, checks your ID and may message you with follow-up questions.

  3. 3Next working day

    Dispensing

    Our GPhC-registered partner pharmacy dispenses your medicine and sends it in a plain, tracked box.

  4. 4Every month

    Ongoing care

    Check-ins, dose reviews and unlimited messages with your clinical team, for as long as you're on treatment.

The clinical review

What your clinician checks

A prescription is a clinical decision. These are the questions your prescriber answers before approving any treatment.

  • Is this the right treatment for your symptoms, or could something else be going on?
  • Is it safe alongside your other medicines and conditions?
  • Is the dose right for you, and when should it change?
  • Do you need a blood test, a photo or a conversation first?
  • Should your GP know, and have you agreed to us telling them?
