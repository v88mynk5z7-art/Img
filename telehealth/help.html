Consultations

How long does a consultation take?

About five minutes. You can stop and come back to it.

Who reviews my answers?

A UK-registered prescriber: a doctor or an independent prescribing pharmacist trained in that treatment area.

Why do you need photo ID?

The law requires us to confirm who you are and that you're over 18 before prescribing. We check ID once, securely.

Do you treat people under 18?

No. All Aumbry services are for adults aged 18 and over.

Prescriptions

What if a clinician says no?

You won't be charged. They'll explain why and suggest what to do next, which may be seeing your GP.

Can I change my dose?

Request a dose review from your account. Your clinician will reply within one working day.

Can I use my NHS prescription with you?

Not at the moment. Aumbry is a private service and issues its own private prescriptions.

Delivery

What does the packaging look like?

A plain, rigid box with no branding and no product names. The sender appears as "AH Dispatch".

How are injection pens kept cold?

They travel in an insulated box with chilled packs, by tracked next-day delivery, and stay at 2–8 °C for up to 48 hours. Put them in your fridge when they arrive.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Not yet. We deliver to addresses in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

Plans and payment

When am I charged?

For prescription treatments, only once a clinician approves your prescription. Plans then renew on the same date each cycle.

How do I pause or cancel?

From your account, at any time before your next renewal date. There are no fees.

Can I return a medicine?

For safety reasons, pharmacies can't take back dispensed medicines unless they're faulty. Skincare and supplements can be returned unopened within 30 days.

Privacy

Who can see my health information?

Only the clinicians and pharmacists involved in your care. We never sell your data. Read our privacy policy.

Will you tell my GP?

Only with your consent, which you can change at any time from your account.

Email care@aumbry.health or use the form. For urgent medical help, call NHS 111, or 999 in an emergency.
