Plans and cancellation

Plans renew automatically until cancelled. You can pause or cancel from your account at any time before the renewal date. Dispensed medicines cannot be returned unless faulty.

Privacy policy

Aumbry Health Ltd is the data controller for the personal and health information you give us. Our Data Protection Officer can be contacted at dpo@yourdomain.

  • We use your information to provide safe clinical care, dispense and deliver your medicines, and run your account.
  • Health data is processed for the provision of healthcare under UK GDPR Article 9(2)(h).
  • We share information with your GP only with your consent, unless there is a serious risk to your safety.
  • We never sell your data. You can request a copy or deletion of your data at any time, subject to medical record-keeping obligations.
