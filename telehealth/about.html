About Aumbry

A clinic that fits around you

An aumbry is the small cabinet set into the wall of old houses and churches, where medicines and precious oils were kept safe and close at hand. We built Aumbry on the same idea: private medicine that is trusted, discreet and always within reach, with clear prices and clinicians who explain their decisions.

Our approach

Medicine first, then convenience

We only offer treatments with strong evidence behind them. Every consultation is written by practising clinicians, every prescription is reviewed by a person, and every patient can reach their clinical team for as long as they're with us.

Principles

What we hold ourselves to

Evidence

Only proven treatments

If the evidence isn't there, we don't sell it. That includes our supplements, which carry only authorised health claims.

Judgement

A clinician decides, every time

No automated approvals. Prescribers can and do say no, and explain why.

Honesty

Clear prices, no hidden fees

The price you see includes your consultation, delivery and ongoing clinical support.

Privacy

Your health stays yours

We never sell data. Your GP is informed only with your consent.
